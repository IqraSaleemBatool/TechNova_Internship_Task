

# Data Visualizations

This folder contains seven visualizations generated during the exploratory data analysis of the Google Play Store dataset.

## 1. Category Distribution

![Category Distribution](Category%20Distribution.png)

Displays the top 10 app categories based on the number of applications.

## 2. Rating Analysis

![Rating Analysis](Rating%20Analysis.png)

Shows the distribution of application ratings and provides an overview of user rating patterns.

## 3. Average Rating by Category

![Average Rating by Category](Average%20Rating%20by%20Category.png)

Compares the top 10 categories based on their average application ratings.

## 4. Installs by Category

![Installs by Category](Installs%20by%20Category.png)

Highlights the top 10 app categories with the highest total installations.

## 5. Free vs Paid Apps

![Free vs Paid Apps](Free%20vs%20Paid%20Apps.png)

Compares the number of free and paid applications in the dataset.

## 6. Reviews vs Installs

![Reviews vs Installs](Reviews%20vs%20Installs.png)

Explores the relationship between application installations and user reviews using logarithmic scales.

## 7. Apps by Year

![Apps by Year](Apps%20by%20Year.png)

Shows the distribution of application records according to their last update year.

---

**Tools Used:** Matplotlib, Seaborn, Pandas, Python.
